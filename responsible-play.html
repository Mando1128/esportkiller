Responsible Play

Use esports projections as entertainment and research, never as guaranteed income. Set firm time and spending limits, never chase losses, and never use money needed for essentials.

If play stops being fun or feels difficult to control, stop and seek support. In the United States, visit NCPG help and treatment resources or call 1-800-MY-RESET.

You must satisfy the minimum age and legal requirements in your location.
