Privacy

Esport Killer stores saved research selections on your own device using browser storage. The current public research board does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Third-party data

The site requests esports schedules, game data, images, and projection data from third-party providers. Those providers may receive standard web request information such as IP address and browser details under their own policies.
