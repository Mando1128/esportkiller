# Esport Killer product roadmap

Updated: September 4, 2026

## Product rule

Every esport uses the same core research experience: current props, upcoming and live matches, active players, teams, recent form, L5/L10/L15/L20, head-to-head history, game logs, over/under selection, and source/update timestamps. Each game adapts that experience to its own mechanics. Do not publish invented lines or statistics.

The platform remains free until CS2 support is complete. Paid subscriptions and the four-player free limit stay disabled until then.

## Planned esport tabs

- League of Legends: champions, builds and items, K/D/A, lane performance, map splits, opponent and team history, and champion/item patch changes.
- CS2: weapons, kills, deaths, assists, headshots, sniper/rifle usage, entry and rush tendencies, map splits, opponent and team history, and weapon/game updates.
- VALORANT: agents, weapons, kills, deaths, assists, first kills/deaths, headshots, role and map splits, opponent and team history, and patch changes.
- Call of Duty: weapons and loadouts, kills, deaths, assists, modes and maps, role/playstyle tendencies, opponent and team history, and balance updates.
- Rocket League: goals, assists, saves, shots, demos, game/series splits, opponent and team history, and game updates.
- Dota 2: heroes, items/builds, K/D/A, last hits, GPM/XPM, roles, opponent and team history, and hero/item patch changes.

## Data and roster policy

- Show only currently active competitive players when a reliable current-season source supports that status.
- Do not describe a player as legally under contract unless a league-recognized contract database or licensed roster provider verifies it.
- Update current teams after reliable transfer or roster announcements; retain former teams only in career history.
- Clearly distinguish live provider data, delayed data, historical data, and unavailable data.
