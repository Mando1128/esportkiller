Data Sources & Freshness

Player history: imported Oracle’s Elixir professional match files. It is historical and labeled cached.

Champion data: Riot Data Dragon and official Riot patch notes.

Schedules/live games: connected League esports providers with a verified schedule snapshot fallback. Device-local time is displayed.

Projections: an unofficial PrizePicks partner feed when reachable. PrizePicks does not provide this project a documented public developer API; feed availability is not guaranteed.

Labels mean: Live was retrieved in the current refresh, Cached is the last verified snapshot, and Unavailable means no trustworthy current response was received. Esport Killer never invents replacement lines or odds.
